# v0.3.9 — #52 Filename Length Cap: Verification Checklist

**Target version:** v0.3.9 (completes #52 on top of the 0.3.8 delimiter-collision work)

**Companion automated tests:** 24 in `tests/one-offs/test_filename_length_cap.py` (16) and `tests/one-offs/test_config_path_robustness.py` (8); full suite 418

## What this is

Hand-runnable verification that over-long session names now produce **working,
truncated** logs instead of silent total loss (#52), and that the caps introduce no
churn or regression for normal names. Sections 1–4 are runnable by an agent; Section 5
is the mutation matrix; Section 6 is live-box-only.
