## Caveats (always)

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Not covered here: the timing of SubagentStop relative to the agent's last write on a live box (AC-2's runtime self-check exists for that and logs `SIDECHAIN-MISMATCH`); the human checklist line reads a live agent's file top to bottom.
